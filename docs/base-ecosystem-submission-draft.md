## Short description (one line)

## Full description

> BasePulse is a free utility dashboard for Base. Paste any Base wallet address to see its
> token portfolio, a gas-fee tracker with 7-day historical guidance on when fees are low,
> and an onchain reputation score broken down by what drives it — Base activity, token
> diversity, L1 history and bridging. Every score page produces a shareable card for X and
> Farcaster. No signup and no wallet connection are needed to look up an address; connect
> only to view your own portfolio or to seal a snapshot of your holdings onchain — a
> keccak256 fingerprint, never the amounts.

## Positioning note (useful context, not a form field)

Base's 2026 pivot named **trading, payments and AI agents** as its three pillars and shut
down the social/creator pillar. BasePulse is a trading-utility dashboard, so it sits in a
pillar that is currently a priority rather than one that was just discontinued. Worth
leaning on if the form has a free-text "why does this fit Base" field.
